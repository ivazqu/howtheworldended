Imperialism is a project, within capitalism, whose power is founded in territorial control and in the ability to control, subjugate, and exploit human and natural resources so as to advance political, economic, or military needs. The command of imperialism determines the well being and wealth of others at the expense of someone else's. This is due to imperialisms ability to accumulate capital and it's ability to feeds off of territories. The accumulation of capital extracts surplus value, an economic prcoess of procuring profit between the wage labourer and the capitalist (see David Harvey 1), and it also takes into consideration the relationship between capitalism and non-capitalistic modes of production, such as the seizure of indigenous land or non-economic practices. Capitalism must always have a force outside of itself, or it will carve a space within itself which could become a part of the working class proletariat, such as the 07'-08' privatization of public services or withdrawing funds from public spending.
