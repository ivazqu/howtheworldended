While Terry Knight was vocal about his pursuits in the energy field, his friends, the Beatles, would be prevalent in multiple oil cross-marketing campaigns with no evident investments in petroleum. In 1964 the beatles’ final show of the Melbourne leg of the world tour was recorded and sponsored by the royal-dutch Shell, thus the hour-long special named The Beatles Sing For Shell. The same year and for their Sydney show, Mobil had put out two promotional flyers for the Beatles concert. In 1968, Shell Oil commissioned a poster that read “All You Need is Shell” to promote the Beatles Yellow Submarine “All You Need Is Love” campaign. In 1979, a special issue of the Help! album was produced for Shell Oil for the kickoff of their 1979 "Shell Helps" campaign. Shell’s [website](https://www.shell.com/who-we-are/our-history/did-you-know.html) mentions the record and their sonic logo being recorded at Abbey Road studios in 2015.
